1. Answer to: How to handle situation where 2 people speak at the same time?
    solution1: use diarization, if having two or more people speaking at once is unavoidable. At worst you'd have to review transcripts manually

    solution2: record every speaker on separate track. This way, even if there are 2 or more people speaking at the same time, you should have clear recordings of everyone involved. IMPORTANT: all recordings should be edited on the same timeline to avoid shifts and mismatches of transcripts

All recordings should be exported as mono track separate for every speaker and using standardized name, e.g. S01E01_John.wav, S01E02_Paul.wav - for an episode with two speakers: John and Paul
